What to do
It's a sandbox: knock over dominoes, blow up Virus blocks, roll as a marble, and build anything you want — alone or with up to 3 friends (Multiplayer).
Party games around the map
Ride the conveyor ring or use the purple teleporters to get around. ⚽ Soccer & 👑 King of the Hill (north) · 🎱 Billiards, 🕹 Arcade, 🪙 Coin Rush, ■ Tile Fall (east) · 🃏 Casino, 🦆 Duck Hunt, 🏀 Basketball, 🎳 Bowling (south) · 🎨 Color Match, 🏁 Obstacle Race, 📺 Cinema (west). Middle: ball pit, TNT, a nuke, crash dummies. Step on a red button to start/reset a game.
