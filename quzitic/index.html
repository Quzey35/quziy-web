Building a drone — Qraft 3D → 🚁 FPV Drone

Pick the frame (freestyle, racing, long range, cinewhoop), size, motors, props, battery and camera tilt. Thrust, top speed, agility and flight time update live. Try the drone course in the Vehicle Park!

🏁 Vehicle Park — step on the green gate next to the start

Race track: cross the checkered line to start the lap timer. Drone course: fly through 8 pink gates in order — a green ring shows the next one. Runway: take off with your planes. The purple pad takes you back.

Party games around the main map

Ride the conveyor ring or use the purple teleporters. ⚽ Soccer & 👑 King of the Hill (north) · 🎱 Billiards, 🕹 Arcade, 🪙 Coin Rush, ■ Tile Fall (east) · 🃏 Casino, 🦆 Duck Hunt, 🏀 Basketball, 🎳 Bowling (south) · 🎨 Color Match, 🏁 Obstacle Race, 📺 Cinema (west). Middle: ball pit, TNT, a nuke, crash dummies. Step on a red button to start a game.
